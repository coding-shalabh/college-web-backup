ST. BENEDICTS ACADEMY

St Benedict’s Academy is a Roman Catholic Benedictine school run by the monks of Asirvanam Benedictine Monastery. It has its roots in the values of the Gospel of Christ and the more than 1500 year old Benedictine tradition of schooling. St Benedict’s Academy entertains students of all religions and honors the dignity of every student as a child of God, and strives to nurture the talents of each individual for service and leadership in their own community and in the larger world.
